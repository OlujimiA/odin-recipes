Jollof Rice

Home

Description

This is a foolproof recipe for cooking Jollof rice, West Africa’s most popular dish. It’s a vibrant, one-pot meal made with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions, and a blend of spices that give it its signature color and depth of flavor. Beloved across the region, Jollof rice is often served at celebrations and gatherings, and this recipe guarantees a delicious, well-balanced result—whether you’re a beginner or an experienced cook.

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups of Rice
  • 1 litre of boiled tomato sauce
  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 1 Onion
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 litre of Chicken Stock
  • 2 Seasoning Cubes
  • 1/4 cup tomato paste
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro, for garnish (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add onion and salt and sauté until onion starts to soften, 4 to 5 minutes
  2. Add tomato paste, and sauté for 2 to 3 minutes.
  3. Add Chicken stock
  4. Turn off the heat, and stir in the rice and add bayleaf
  5. Turn heat to high, cover with a tight fitting lid, reduce heat to medium-low, and simmer for 20 minutes.
  6. After the 12 minutes are up, remove lid. Serve garnished with green onions and chopped cilantro.
